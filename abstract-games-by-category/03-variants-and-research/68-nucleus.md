# Nucleus

## Type
Syllabic letter-grid word game.

## Players
2 or more

## Setup
A grid containing randomized letters or consonant-vowel clusters.

## Turn
Players search for valid words whose core root spans connected adjacent cells on the board, scoring points proportional to word length.

## History
Published in 1983 by Giorgio Cecchetti in the Italian puzzle magazine *Pergioco*.
