# Poe

## Type
Secret word deduction and anagram game.

## Players
2

## Goal
Deduce the opponent's secret 5-letter word with the fewest guesses.

## Turn
1. Each player chooses a secret 5-letter word.
2. Players take turns calling out 5-letter test words (guesses).
3. The opponent scores the guess based on how many letters match the secret word and whether anagrammatic permutations exist.

## History
Invented by legendary game designer Sid Sackson (author of *A Gamut of Games*), named in honor of Edgar Allan Poe.
