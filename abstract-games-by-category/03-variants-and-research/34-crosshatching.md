# Crosshatching

## Type
Line / grid game.

## Status
The name is used for several paper games.

## Recommended catalog treatment
Keep this entry as a research candidate until the exact historical ruleset is selected.

## Candidate mechanic
Players draw line segments on a grid to create or claim enclosed regions.

## Implementation
Reuse the edge/region engine from Dots and Boxes, but make:
- legal edge directions;
- scoring region;
- turn continuation

configuration-driven.

## Warning
Do not merge this with Dots and Boxes automatically; the names describe related but not necessarily identical games.
