# Mishmash

## Type
Word substring / lexical puzzle game.

## Players
2 (or more)

## Goal
Propose letter cores that your opponent cannot find valid words for, while finding valid words for cores proposed to you.

## Turn
1. Player 1 proposes a "core" (a sequence of 2 or 3 letters, e.g. `SQ` or `GHT`).
2. Player 2 must name an English (or Spanish) dictionary word that contains those letters in that exact contiguous sequence (e.g. `MASQUERADE`, `SQUARE`).
3. If Player 2 cannot find a word, Player 1 must prove a valid word exists. If Player 1 proves it, Player 1 gains points; if Player 1 cannot, Player 1 is penalized.

## History
Invented in 1882 by Lewis Carroll (Rev. Charles Lutwidge Dodgson), author of *Alice in Wonderland*.
