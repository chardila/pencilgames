# Word Hunt & Verbal Rope

## Type
Word search and lexical chaining games.

## Players
2 or more

## Word Hunt (Caccia alla Parola)
- Players agree on a sentence or master word.
- Within a time limit, players extract as many sub-words as possible using the letters available.

## Verbal Rope (Corda Verbale)
- Player 1 writes a word with at least 4 letters.
- Player 2 has 10 seconds to write a new word that starts with the last 2 letters of Player 1's word.
- If a player fails within 10 seconds, they receive a penalty strike; 3 strikes eliminates a player.

## History
Traditional word and parlor games recorded in Andrea Angiolino's *Super Sharp Pencil & Paper Games*.
