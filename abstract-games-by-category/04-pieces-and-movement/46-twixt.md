# TwixT (candidate — deferred)

## Status
Deferred. Connection game on a 24×24 grid: players place pegs and join their own non-adjacent
pegs with knight's-move "bridges", racing to link their two sides. Bridges may not cross.

## Why not now
papg.com has no full ruleset for it, and the bridge-crossing rules are fiddly to adjudicate on a
paper-style board. Revisit only if explicitly prioritised; needs a chosen canonical ruleset first.
