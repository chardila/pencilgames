# Horsey / Blockades (Cavalluccio)

## Players
2

## Goal
Trap the opponent's piece so that it has no legal knight moves remaining.

## Setup
A grid of squares (e.g. $8 \times 8$) or hexagonal tiles. Each player places their "Horsey" (Knight) token on opposite starting squares.

## Turn
1. Move your Knight using the standard Chess Knight L-move ($2$ squares in one orthogonal direction, $1$ square perpendicular).
2. The destination square must be unoccupied and unvisited.
3. The square just vacated by the knight is marked with an **X** (blocked/burned) and can never be entered by either player for the rest of the game.

## End
The first player who cannot make a legal Knight move on their turn loses.

## History
Traditional European pencil-and-paper game, originally known as *Blockades* or *Cavalluccio*.
