# Kropki (Dots)

## Players
2

## Goal
Capture more of the opponent's dots by enclosing them.

## Setup
Use a grid of intersections.

## Turn
Place one dot of your color on an unoccupied intersection.

When a closed region containing opponent dots is completely enclosed by the player's dots/lines according to the Kropki rules, the enclosed opponent dots are captured.

## Territory variants
Kropki has multiple rulesets, including variants concerning territory, enclosure, and liberation of previously captured dots.

## Implementation warning
Do not implement a generic “flood fill equals capture” rule without first choosing the exact Kropki ruleset.

## Source
Kropki rules documentation describes realms formed by enclosure and captured opponent dots.
