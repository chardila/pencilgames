# The Walls (Le Mura)

## Players
2

## Goal
Trap the opponent so they cannot continue drawing the Chinese Wall without intersecting an existing wall.

## Setup
1. Players collaboratively draw a winding continuous boundary line dividing the paper into 20 to 30 closed irregular regions.

## Turn
Taking turns, players extend a continuous "Chinese Wall" line:
- The wall starts at the paper boundary and traverses from region to region.
- The wall must cross region borders and cannot cross itself, previously drawn walls, or double back through the same region boundary twice.

## End
The player who cannot legally extend the wall without causing an illegal intersection loses.

## History
Traditional spatial topological game documented by Andrea Angiolino.
