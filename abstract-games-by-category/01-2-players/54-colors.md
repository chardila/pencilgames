# Colors

## Players
2 (or more)

## Goal
Score the most points (or make the last legal placement) while coloring nodes/regions on a planar graph using 4 colors.

## Setup
A pre-drawn map or connected planar graph. 4 distinct colors are available to all players.

## Turn
A player chooses an uncolored zone and assigns one of the 4 colors such that no adjacent zone shares that color.

## Variant: Cartographer (Silverman, 1971)
- Player 1 acts as the *Cartographer* (drawing new adjacent regions).
- Player 2 acts as the *Colorist* (assigning one of the 4 colors to the newly created region).
- The Colorist wins if all regions are colored without ever requiring a 5th color; the Cartographer wins if they force a configuration where 4 colors are insufficient.

## History
Created in 1971 by David L. Silverman; related to Stephen Barr's *Regions*.
