# The Flat / Ululo (Il Piatto)

## Players
2

## Goal
Complete 5-in-a-row lines on an open grid or torus, or complete target letter sequences in the Ululo variant.

## Setup
Played on graph paper. One player uses `X`, the other uses `O`.

## Turn
Players mark an intersection or square. Each completed 5-in-a-row scores 1 point and is crossed out so it cannot be reused.

## Variant: Ululo (Angiolino, 1995)
- Player 1 writes letters **U**, **L**, or **O** on any empty square.
- Player 2 writes letters **A**, **M**, or **I** on any empty square.
- Player 1 wins by forming the word **ULULO** in any direction (orthogonal or diagonal, forward or reverse).
- Player 2 wins by forming the word **AMAMI** in any direction.

## History
Known in Italy as *Il Piatto* / *The Game of the Five Crosses*.
