# Y

## Players
2

## Goal
Connect all three sides of a triangular board using one continuous chain of your stones.

## Setup
Use a triangular board of hexagonal cells. Side length N has N(N+1)/2 cells
(NOT N×N). Recommended sizes: N = 7, 9, or 11 (28, 45, 66 cells).

Each player is assigned one color.

## Turn
Place one stone of your color on any empty cell.

## Victory
A player wins when a single connected chain of their stones touches all three
sides of the triangle at once. The three sides are goals for BOTH players
(unlike Hex, where each player owns one pair of edges).

## Indexing & connectivity
Row `r` (0..N-1, apex to base) holds cells `c` in 0..r; `index = r*(r+1)/2 + c`.
Neighbors of `(r,c)`: `(r,c±1)`, `(r-1,c-1)`, `(r-1,c)`, `(r+1,c)`, `(r+1,c+1)`,
each bounds-checked as `0 <= r' <= N-1` and `0 <= c' <= r'`.
Sides: left = `c === 0`, right = `c === r`, bottom = `r === N-1`.

## Implementation
On each placement, flood-fill from the just-placed stone only, across same-color
neighbors. Track which of the three sides that one component touches; the mover
wins iff it touches all three. Do NOT seed the search from every cell of one
side at once — that merges disjoint components and reports false wins.

## Note
Y is closely related to Hex but has a three-sided connection objective and never
draws (a full board always has exactly one winner).
