# Y

## Players
2

## Goal
Connect all three sides of a triangular board using one continuous chain of your stones.

## Setup
Use a hexagonal-cell triangular board. Recommended: 5×5 or 7×7.

Each player is assigned one color.

## Turn
Place one stone of your color on any empty cell.

## Victory
A player wins when their stones form a connected component touching all three sides of the board.

## Connectivity
Use the six neighboring cells of the hexagonal grid.

## Implementation
BFS from all cells touching one side. A player wins if the reachable component touches the other two sides as well.

## Note
Y is closely related to Hex but has a three-sided connection objective.
