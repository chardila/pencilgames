# War of the Stars (Guerra delle Stelle / Footsteps / Quo Vadis)

## Players
2 (or 4 players in 2 teams)

## Goal
Capture all star figures from the opponent or have more stars when funds expire.

## Setup
- Each player starts with **$60 million** bankroll and **3 stars** (represented as stick figures).

## Turn (Simultaneous Blind Bidding)
1. Privately write down a bid between $1 million and your remaining available money.
2. Both players reveal their bids simultaneously.
3. Both players deduct their bid amount from their bankroll regardless of who won.
4. **Resolution:**
   - **Higher bid:** Wins a star! Draws a new stick figure in their column; the loser crosses out one of their stars.
   - **Tie bid:** Neither player gains or loses a star (money is still deducted).
5. If a player runs out of money ($0 remaining), the other player can win remaining stars with bids of only $1M.

## End
The game ends when one player loses all 3 stars (loses immediately) or both run out of funds (most stars wins).

## History
Traditional British simultaneous auction game known as *Footsteps*, adapted into the commercial board game *Quo Vadis*.
