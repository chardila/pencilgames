# Synapse (Sinapsi)

## Players
2

## Goal
Build a connected neural/synaptic network from your starting node to the target output node while severing or redirecting the opponent's network.

## Setup
A grid of synaptic nodes ($6 \times 6$ or $8 \times 8$). Player 1 owns the North-South nodes, Player 2 owns the East-West nodes.

## Turn
A player connects two neighboring nodes with a directed or undirected axon/synapse line of their color, or activates a synaptic node.
Lines may not cross opponent active lines.

## End
First player to establish an unbroken pathway of synapses from their source to their target wins.

## History
Invented by French mathematician and puzzle author Pierre Berloquin in 1976.
