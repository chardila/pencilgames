# The Squared Map (La Mappa Quadrettata)

## Players
2

## Goal
Claim more territory on a grid map through 4-color constraints.

## Setup
A grid of squares (e.g. $8 \times 8$ or $10 \times 10$) on graph paper. 4 colors available.

## Turn
Players take turns claiming an uncolored square and filling it with one of the 4 colors.
- Orthogonally adjacent squares cannot share the same color.
- A player who completes a $2 \times 2$ block of 4 distinct colors scores bonus points.

## End
The game ends when no more legal squares can be colored. The player with the highest score / most colored cells wins.

## History
Published in 1989 by Italian game designer Pietro Gorini.
