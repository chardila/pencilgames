# Lasso

## Players
2

## Goal
Capture more points/territory by looping closed line paths ("lassos") around dots on a grid.

## Setup
A grid of dots (e.g. $6 \times 6$).

## Turn
1. Place a pin/dot on an intersection, or
2. Extend a continuous line segment connecting dots to form a closed loop.
3. Any enemy dots captured entirely inside the closed loop are claimed and score points.

## History
Invented by British board game designer Eric Solomon (creator of *Black Box*).
