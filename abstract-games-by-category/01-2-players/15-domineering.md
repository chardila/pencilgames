# Domineering

## Players
2

## Goal
Make the last legal move.

## Setup
Use a rectangular grid.

Player 1 places vertical dominoes.
Player 2 places horizontal dominoes.

## Turn
A vertical player must cover two vertically adjacent empty cells.

A horizontal player must cover two horizontally adjacent empty cells.

## End
If the current player has no legal placement, that player loses.

## Difference from Cram
Cram allows either player to place either orientation. Domineering assigns one orientation to each player.
