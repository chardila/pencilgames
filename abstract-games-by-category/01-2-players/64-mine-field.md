# Mine Field (Campo Minato / Sturmtruppen)

## Players
2

## Goal
Infiltrate your scout troops across the enemy's hidden minefield to reach the opposite border without detonating hidden mines.

## Setup
Each player has two $10 \times 10$ grids:
1. *Defensive Field:* Where the player secretly places 10-15 hidden mines and anti-tank traps.
2. *Radar / Tracking Grid:* Where the player records reconnaissance and moves through the enemy's field.

## Turn
On each turn, a player moves their soldier 1 step orthogonally or declares a sweep. The opponent announces whether the entered cell is Clear, a Mine (soldier eliminated), or a Warning (adjacent to a mine).

## End
First player to guide their troop to the enemy's back row wins.

## History
Created by Giorgio Corbetta and Mario Gomboli (1979) in *Paper and Pencil Battles with the Sturmtruppen*.
