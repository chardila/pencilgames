# Engel

## Players
2

## Goal
Be the last player able to draw a legal directed connection between squares in the same row or column.

## Setup
A $5 \times 5$ square grid on graph paper.

## Turn
A player selects two empty squares in the same row or column and draws a straight connecting line segment between them.
- Line segments in the same row or column cannot overlap or cross previously drawn connections.

## End
When a player has no legal pair of squares to connect in any row or column, that player loses.

## History
Invented in 1975 by German mathematician Arthur Engel.
