# Wild Cats (Gatti Selvaggi)

## Players
2

## Goal
Form a line of 4 of your symbols (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) on a $5 \times 5$ grid.

## Setup
A $5 \times 5$ grid with rows numbered $1..5$ and columns lettered $A..E$. Player 1 uses `X`, Player 2 uses `O`.

## Turn (Simultaneous Secret Action)
1. Both players secretly and simultaneously write down the coordinates of an empty cell (e.g. `C3`).
2. Both players reveal their choices simultaneously:
   - **Different cells:** Both players mark their respective symbols in their chosen cells.
   - **Same cell (Clash):** Neither symbol is placed; the square remains empty for that round. Players cannot immediately choose that same square on the very next round.

## Constraint
A player who already has 3 symbols in a row cannot pick the 4th winning square if it is the only winning completion available—their opponent must be given the opportunity to contest or block.
